Privacy, in plain language.

Move Confirmed is designed so appointment and customer data stays on the device where you enter it.

What stays local

Appointment titles, customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, change history, business defaults, and acknowledgement receipts are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. We do not run an application server that receives or stores them. Removing site data in your browser removes these records unless you exported a backup.

What a shared link contains

A confirmation link carries the appointment change, customer first name, business return contact, expiry, and a random verification token in the URL fragment after #. Browsers do not send fragments in ordinary web requests. The link intentionally excludes the customer’s phone number and email address. Anyone who receives the link can read its contents until it expires, so send it only to the intended customer.

Messages and acknowledgement

The app opens your device’s SMS or email composer. It does not send messages itself and does not read their delivery status. A customer acknowledgement creates a return receipt link; the business must open and accept that receipt on the original device before its local log changes.

Purchase and license checks

If you buy Plus, checkout is hosted by Sociobot and Dodo, the merchant of record. Their checkout privacy terms apply to payment details. This app stores the returned license token in local storage and sends only that token to the Sociobot verification endpoint, at most once daily under normal use. Payment card details never pass through this app.

Network and analytics

There are no advertising trackers, behavioral analytics, third-party fonts, or runtime CDNs. The hosting platform may keep standard security and request logs. The service worker caches app files on your device for offline use.

Your controls

Export JSON or CSV from the workbench at any time. Delete individual records from the local log, or clear this site’s storage in browser settings. For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the repository linked in the app footer.
